# SWARAJ SAATHI
## Voice-Native AI Platform for Rural Welfare Discovery
### Made for Viksit Bharat 🇮🇳 by Sarthak Rodge

---

## 🔴 The Problem

India has **700+ government welfare schemes** across Central and State levels. Yet:

- **60%** of eligible citizens never discover the schemes meant for them
- **42%** of rural Indians cannot navigate English-only digital portals
- **Middlemen** exploit citizens by charging ₹500–₹2,000 just to fill forms
- Scheme data is **scattered** across 100+ disconnected government websites

> *"The schemes exist. The money exists. The citizens just can't reach them."*

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Swaraj Saathi is not a chatbot. It is a **Digital Government Clerk** on your phone.

| What citizens do today | What Swaraj Saathi does |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| Visit multiple websites | One app, one conversation |
| Fill complex English forms | Just talk in your language |
| Pay middlemen for help | Free, autonomous AI assistant |
| Wait weeks for status | Instant tracking in chat |
| Miss schemes they qualify for | AI proactively matches schemes |

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 1. Swaraj Sahayak (AI Chat Assistant)
Citizens simply type or speak naturally — *"मुझे शिकायत दर्ज करनी है"* — and the AI **autonomously** handles the entire process step-by-step. No forms. No navigation. Just conversation.

### 2. Magic Auto-Match
A profile-based eligibility engine that instantly matches citizens to every scheme they qualify for, based on age, income, caste, occupation, and location.

### 3. Rakshak (Grievance System)
File complaints, upload evidence photos, and track resolution — all from within the app. Every ticket gets a unique ID and status updates.

### 4. Multilingual & Voice-First
Full support for **Hindi**, **Marathi**, and **English** — with voice input/output powered by **Sarvam AI**, India's own language AI.

### 5. Automated Data Pipeline
Our system **automatically scrapes** government portals daily to keep scheme data current — no manual database updates needed.

---

## 🚀 What Makes Us Different

### Agentic AI (Not a Chatbot)
Traditional chatbots answer questions. **Our AI takes action.**

When a citizen says *"I want to file a complaint"*, the system:
- Detects the intent automatically
- Identifies what information is missing
- Asks for each piece conversationally
- Executes the task in the backend
- Returns a confirmation receipt — all in the chat

This is the **Agentic AI paradigm** — the next evolution beyond chatbots.

### Privacy-by-Design
We use **Sarvam AI** — an indigenous Indian language model — running **entirely on local servers**. 

- ❌ No OpenAI. No Google Cloud. No data leaving India.
- ✅ 100% sovereign data processing
- ✅ Compliant with government data localization mandates
- ✅ Works even without internet (on-premise deployment)

### One-Command Deployment
The entire platform — AI engine, databases, APIs — is **containerized**. Any government office can deploy it with a single command. No DevOps team needed.
